Original call and provenance

Saving fate decisions leaves the source TIFFs, baseline PI, condition and original call intact. Splitting creates two new PI measurements from your masks and preserves the mixed original. Exports include the project identity and current decisions; the project folder retains the decision history. Changes to the timing or decision affect the next regenerated results.

How to interpret the images

After tracking becomes uncertain, image windows are context only. An outline is an algorithmic assignment, not proof of identity. First visible division can occur after growth begins. Not-grown requires reliable observation throughout the full recording; loss, blur and overlap are unresolved.

The baseline PI measurement stays fixed when you revise a call. The split tool remeasures PI separately for each new initial cell. Drawing an outline on another frame is a tracking annotation and does not change baseline PI.
